FitLife: Beginner Fitness Guide

Site Name

FitLife: Beginner Fitness Guide was chosen because it represents a website dedicated to helping beginners improve their health through exercise, nutrition, and fitness education.

Optional Domain: fitlifeguide.org

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide beginner-friendly fitness resources, workout routines, healthy lifestyle tips, and interactive tools that encourage healthier living. Visitors will learn basic fitness concepts and use practical resources such as a BMI calculator and workout planner.

Scenarios

  • What type of workout is best for beginners?
  • How can I calculate my BMI?
  • What exercises can I do at home without equipment?
  • How do I create a weekly workout plan?
  • How can I save my favorite workouts?
